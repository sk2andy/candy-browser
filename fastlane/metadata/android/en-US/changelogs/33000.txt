Candy Sync adds self-hosted, end-to-end encrypted tab sync across Android, Chromium, and Firefox. Federated login compatibility can allow third-party cookies temporarily for the current tab or persistently for the site in its profile. Also new: Candy Recall, external link previews, page translation, configurable toolbar parking, and a startup-animation setting. Fixes improve popups, HTML links, force dark, frosted blur, compact URLs, and edge-to-edge scrolling.
