Candy Browser is a gesture-first, Material 3 Expressive browser for Android 13 and newer. It uses GeckoView by default, supports compatible Mozilla-signed Firefox add-ons, and keeps tabs, history, favorites, profiles, site permissions, reader saves, and privacy telemetry on your device.

Highlights:
• Swipe between tabs and open a visual cover-flow, grid, or list overview.
• Install compatible Firefox add-ons and manage them directly in Candy.
• Block ads, trackers, and cookie banners with uBlock Origin and Candy's local protection.
• Inspect blocked requests and site permissions in Privacy X-Ray and Permission Radar.
• Use private tabs that keep their session and journey data in memory only.
• Read and save articles locally with Reader Studio and text-to-speech.
• Organize browsing with profiles, Candy Trails, favorites, snoozed tabs, and Site Capsules.
• Customize compatible sites with reviewed, explicitly installed Toppings.
• Switch to Android System WebView when you prefer the system-managed engine.
• Choose from several search engines or configure a SearXNG instance.

Remote search suggestions are disabled by default and can be enabled explicitly in settings. Candy is open source under the Mozilla Public License 2.0.
