# Connect your pages. Make room for guests.

Candy Browser 0.47.0 introduces Candy Constellations, a local map of related pages, and Guest Bubble,
a separate browsing space for lending someone your phone. New Android integrations bring selected
website snapshots, saved articles, and personal link rules closer to the places you already use them.
Reader Studio and everyday navigation get a more comfortable touch, too.

## Discover connections with Candy Constellations

Open **Candy Constellations** from the browser menu to explore related pages in the current profile.
Pan and zoom through the map, select a page to see its excerpt and related pages, or search saved
titles, URLs, and excerpts to jump between matches.

- Enable Constellations separately for each regular local profile. Maps stay within their profile.
- Download the optional model once, then page analysis runs on your device. Page content is never
  sent to a model service.
- New readable visits build the map; Candy does not fetch your old history in the background.
- Private tabs and temporary previews are excluded. History deletion removes the corresponding map
  entries; disabling Constellations clears that profile's map. Maps are excluded from sync and backup.

> Setup requires an explicit model download of about 138 MB from Hugging Face. Connections indicate
> topic similarity, not agreement or factual accuracy.

Learn more about [Candy Constellations](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.47.0/docs/browsing/README.md).

## Lend Candy with Guest Bubble

Enable **Guest Bubble** under **Settings → Tabs & gestures**, then long-press the profile **+** button
to hand over a separate, temporary browser. Your Candy profiles, tabs, history, and settings stay
behind the owner lock. Returning to your browser requires Android device authentication; Candy
stops the guest session and clears its website data before unlocking.

Guest Bubble requires profiles and an available strong biometric or device screen lock. It uses a
separate System WebView session, without your cookies, extensions, or Toppings. Downloads, file
uploads, website permissions, and external-app navigation are unavailable in the guest session.

> Guest Bubble protects Candy's own surfaces. It does not lock Android or other apps and is not
> Android screen pinning or a device guest account.

Learn more about [Guest Bubble](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.47.0/docs/tabs-and-profiles/README.md).

## Bring useful parts of the web to Android

![Candy's website-widget crop editor alongside a website snapshot placed on the Android home screen](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/v0.47.0/docs/screenshots/candy-website-widget-prototype.png)

- **Website widgets, now as a prototype:** choose an area of the visible page, crop it to a home-screen
  grid, and refresh it manually or on a schedule. Tap the snapshot to reopen its source in Candy.
  Widgets display images; website links, forms, and scrolling open the browser. Android may delay
  scheduled refreshes, and layout changes can require selecting the area again.
- **Site Capsules in their own windows:** optionally give a Capsule a separate Android task and
  Recents card, with its own tab session and the Capsule's name and icon.
- **Research or listen to selected text:** compatible Android apps can offer **Research in Candy**
  with an explicit profile choice, or **Read aloud with Candy** for a temporary speech session.
- **Attach saved reading:** Android's document picker can expose eligible saved Reader articles as
  offline, text-only PDFs, alongside completed Candy downloads. Older items without recorded source
  ownership remain available inside Candy.
- **Personal link rules:** send incoming domains to a chosen profile, Reader Studio, or a Site
  Capsule. Rules are optional and apply to links Android opens in Candy.
- **Optional device search:** explicitly select bookmarks and saved articles for system search.
  Requires Android 13 or later and a compatible search provider or launcher; availability varies.

Find the [Android integration guides](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.47.0/docs/browsing/README.md)
and [Site Capsule window details](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.47.0/docs/site-capsules/README.md).

## Settle into Reader Studio

Reader Studio gives the article more space, with floating controls that compact as you read and
expand when you scroll back. Open the settings sheet for typography, alignment, colors, offline
saving, and speech controls, or hide the controls completely. Back brings hidden controls back first.

- Choose serif or sans-serif text. Paper is the default for new Reader settings; existing theme
  choices stay intact.
- Native predictive Back now extends to more Candy screens, including Reader Studio.
- The browser menu now opens as a bottom sheet, with fixed shortcuts and browser controls.
  Choose a scrolling or swipeable layout, customize its actions, or switch back to Floating menu
  under **Tabs & gestures → Menu actions**.
- Enable **Switch address pill with swipes** under **Tabs & gestures** to compact or expand the
  address pill with viewport drags, including on pages that do not scroll. This option starts off.
- Configure Link Peek's right-swipe action to suit your workflow; a left swipe still closes it.
- Pick the new light or dark Candy Outline launcher icons.
- Candy Sync settings offer links to the Chrome and Firefox companion extension listings, ready
  to share with another device.

Learn more about [Reader Studio](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.47.0/docs/reader-studio/README.md)
and [appearance and controls](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.47.0/docs/browsing/README.md).

## Try upcoming changes alongside Stable

Nightly builds now use separate app identities, so you can keep Stable installed while trying
upcoming changes. Each channel keeps its own data. The download guide includes separate Obtainium
links for Stable and Nightly, with variant-specific update filters.

See the [download and update guide](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.47.0/README.md#download).

## Smoother navigation and more precise site cleanup

- Opening an external-link preview as a full tab preserves its live page, including form input,
  scroll position, and navigation history.
- Address completion works again with **Go** and **Enter**. Automatic parking respects the chosen
  edge, and home-page favorites stay above the keyboard controls.
- Gecko better respects nested scrolling during pull-to-refresh, with fixes for page navigation,
  external-app lookups, and safe-area positioning.
- **Clear this site's cookies & data** now works with Gecko while respecting the current storage
  context and profile boundaries. The confirmation explains the domain scope and shared storage.

> Gecko keeps its HTTP cache because its site-cache API cannot safely filter by Candy profile.
> Other tabs sharing the affected storage context may also be signed out of that site.

Learn more about [site data deletion](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.47.0/docs/browsing/README.md).
