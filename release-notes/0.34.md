# Quick actions and reliable browsing

Candy Sync keeps your Android and desktop tabs together, while faster shortcuts and focused browsing
fixes make everyday navigation smoother.

![A synced desktop device selected as a writable Candy Browser profile](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/v0.34/docs/screenshots/candy-sync-device-profile.png)

## Candy Sync across devices

- Connect Candy Browser, Chromium, and Firefox through your self-hosted Candy Sync server.
- Use every connected device as a writable profile: open, navigate, reorder, pin, or close tabs from another device.
- Changes are encrypted before leaving each device, delivered through retry-safe REST and authenticated WebSocket updates, and recovered automatically after reconnecting.
- Your server stores ciphertext; passphrases, workspace keys, private keys, URLs, titles, device names, and icons never reach it in plaintext.

Learn more in [Cross-device sync](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.34/README.md#cross-device-sync).

## Faster actions

- Long-press Candy's app icon to open a regular tab, open a private tab, or jump to a recent inactive local profile.
- Launcher shortcuts now use compact black glyphs on light neutral surfaces for a cleaner Android menu.
- Link Peek now places **Copy**, **Open in private tab**, and **Share** beside the pulsing new-tab target. Implements [#61](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/issues/61).

Learn more in [Tabs and profiles](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.34/docs/tabs-and-profiles/README.md#topic-lookup) and [Link Peek actions](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.34/docs/browsing/address-actions-and-ui.md#gestures-and-actions).

## Fixes and polish

- Tapped HTTP(S) links now stay in Candy instead of being handed to Android's external-app resolver. Fixes [#55](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/issues/55).
- The private-mode toggle stays beside the address field while editing a blank tab.
- Candy Sync's device setup controls have clearer spacing.
- Candy now presents these release highlights once after each app update; fresh installs continue through normal onboarding.

## Private by design

Private tabs remain memory-only. Private activity never affects recent-profile launcher shortcuts, and synced device profiles are never published to the Android launcher.
