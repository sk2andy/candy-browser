# Clearer loading, smoother browsing

Candy Browser 0.44.0 adds a theme-colored loading indicator and Gopeed download support, with fixes
for page loading, app links, website layouts, and Gecko push notifications. The Android Firefox
engine also moves to GeckoView 157.

## See real page-loading progress

- The address-bar border now follows progress reported by the active browser engine. When progress
  is not yet available, it shows an indeterminate indicator instead of an invented percentage.
- Choose **Tonal** in Appearance for a loading border that uses your theme colors and a soft halo.
  **Rainbow** remains the default, and both styles respect your animation setting.
- Loading and reload transitions are more reliable, with fewer blank surfaces and stale loading
  states. Light and dark appearance also reconcile correctly when returning to Candy.

Learn more about [loading styles](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.44.0/docs/browsing/appearance-and-settings.md#address-loading-styles).

## Downloads reach the right app

- Installed **Gopeed** now appears among the supported external download managers and can receive
  downloads directly from Candy.
- Open a downloaded APK from Candy to continue in Android's package installer. Android may ask you
  to allow Candy as an installation source; each installation still needs your approval.
- Generic download handlers no longer intercept ordinary website links intended for matching apps.
  Google Play links from search results open correctly, and returning from an app handoff preserves
  the source tab.

![Candy's Downloads screen with an APK transfer, file search, and recent downloads](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/v0.44.0/docs/screenshots/downloads-overview.png)

Learn more about [download behavior](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.44.0/docs/browsing/downloads.md#behavior) and [app handoffs](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.44.0/docs/browsing/runtime-and-navigation.md#navigation-paths).

## Pages fit and respond better

- Pull to refresh works across the page again when you are at the top. Instagram, TikTok, and
  YouTube keep a limited top gesture area to protect their video feeds and nested scrolling.
- Full-screen dialogs, search editors, and top-edge controls better respect the status-bar safe
  area while keeping buttons tappable.
- System WebView respects mobile viewport directives, helping affected sites keep their intended
  phone layout.
- The main menu uses standard Material placement, and site protection has a compact icon action
  that still opens connection details and privacy controls.

Learn more about [page and gesture behavior](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.44.0/docs/browsing/runtime-and-navigation.md#invariants) and [site protection](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.44.0/docs/browsing/address-actions-and-ui.md#site-protection-icon).

## Gecko push works while you browse

Website service workers can initialize correctly, and Gecko push notifications keep arriving while
Candy is in the foreground, including after idle periods, without restarting the app. Delivery uses
Mozilla Autopush and does not require Google Play services.

> Background delivery still depends on Android scheduling and battery restrictions. System WebView
> has no website push integration, and private tabs do not create persistent push subscriptions.

Learn more about [website push](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.44.0/docs/browsing/web-push.md).
