# More control, without losing what makes Candy yours

Candy Browser 0.41.2 brings new privacy controls, calmer browsing, and more deliberate tab and
history cleanup. It also carries forward the complete 0.41 experience: Candy Player, Android media
controls, customizable address chrome, tablet tabs, and the privacy protections introduced in the
main release.

## Set clearer privacy boundaries

![Candy Browser privacy controls](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/v0.41.2/docs/screenshots/candy-privacy.png)

- Reduce browser-identifying details in the user agent, select a WebRTC IP-protection mode, and
  block WebRTC completely when a site does not need it.
- Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are on by default. Candy sends their request headers and
  exposes matching browser signals to pages; either setting can be changed independently.
- Candy bypasses trusted Google AMP and AMP Cache wrappers by default, taking you to the publisher's
  original page while retaining an opt-out for sites that need the wrapper.

> WebRTC blocking or restrictive IP handling can prevent voice and video features from working on
> some sites. System WebView uses its available boundary: any non-standard WebRTC mode blocks peer
> connections rather than claiming equivalent IP-handling support.

Learn more in [Privacy protection](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41.2/docs/privacy-protection/README.md#topic-lookup), [engine boundaries](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41.2/docs/browsing/platform-engines.md#implemented-boundary), and [navigation paths](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41.2/docs/browsing/runtime-and-navigation.md#navigation-paths).

## Let motion match your pace

- Turn Candy animations off globally from Appearance. Candy's own transitions and startup motion
  stop, while direct gesture movement remains immediate and controllable.
- The same preference is applied to active web pages in GeckoView and System WebView, so page motion
  is suppressed consistently after the browser policy is ready.

Learn more in [Appearance choices](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41.2/docs/browsing/appearance-and-settings.md#choices) and [animation suppression](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41.2/docs/browsing/platform-engines.md#android-gecko-and-extension-invariants).

## Keep tabs and history on your terms

- Choose separate cleanup behavior for backgrounding and for closing Candy from Android Recents.
  Protected sign-in windows keep their active session, and Candy leaves a fresh blank tab when a
  configured cleanup clears the active profile.
- History can retain individual visits and offer a Distinct URLs view when repeated entries would
  otherwise obscure the pages you want to find.
- Add Home as a configurable address-bar action for faster access to your start page.

Learn more in [History behavior](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41.2/docs/browsing/history.md#behavior), [tab lifecycle](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41.2/docs/tabs-and-profiles/lifecycle-and-persistence.md#model-and-policy), and [address-bar actions](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41.2/docs/browsing/address-actions-and-ui.md#configurable-expanded-actions).

## Watch videos with Candy Player

- Choose whether Candy Player appears from a button, works inline as well as fullscreen, takes over
  controls in supported website fullscreen, or starts when a supported video begins playing.
- Enter Android picture-in-picture directly from a recognized inline video. Fullscreen and PiP
  returns restore the same page player and its position.
- Candy stays attached to the original Gecko session; it does not open or download a second media
  stream.

> Candy Player targets visible top-frame HTML video in GeckoView. Cross-origin embeds, canvas-only
> players, DRM restrictions, and hostile site scripts remain unsupported or best effort.

Learn more in [Picture-in-picture and Candy Player](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41.2/docs/browsing/picture-in-picture.md#supported-paths).

## Keep playback connected to Android

- Eligible live Gecko playback can continue after Home or screen lock through a media-playback
  foreground service.
- Android's media notification and system surfaces control the exact playing session with Play,
  Pause, Stop, and Seek when the page reports a finite duration.
- Private tabs, locked profiles, closed pages, and stale navigations cannot publish metadata or
  keep control of the media session.

Learn more in [Android system controls and background playback](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41.2/docs/browsing/picture-in-picture.md#android-system-controls-and-background-playback).

## Make the address bar yours

- Select the classic or segmented address-bar style, then choose Theme, Dimmed, Graphite, Black, or
  a validated custom color.
- Configure address-bar long press for Link Peek, copy, share, download, or foreground/background
  tabs—including private tabs.
- On windows at least 600 dp wide, the unfocused address field becomes a scrollable tab strip with
  favicons or title initials.

Learn more in [Appearance choices](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41.2/docs/browsing/appearance-and-settings.md#choices), [address flow](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41.2/docs/browsing/address-actions-and-ui.md#address-flow), and [Tabs and profiles](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41.2/docs/tabs-and-profiles/README.md#topic-lookup).

## Keep existing browsing handoffs reliable

- Sites with a third-party-cookie exception receive it before bookmark, unloaded-tab, reload, and
  restored-session navigation begins.
- Pull to refresh accepts downward gestures starting within the top safe area, without taking over
  lower page content.
- External Link Peek previews survive temporary switches such as opening a password manager;
  explicit launcher opens still discard the preview.
- Candy download progress and completion notifications work again, including safe capture of
  user-opened same-origin generated `blob:` popups in System WebView.

Learn more in [runtime and navigation](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41.2/docs/browsing/runtime-and-navigation.md#invariants) and [download behavior](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41.2/docs/browsing/downloads.md#behavior).

Private tabs remain memory-only. Candy Player state and Android media ownership are also memory-only
and are discarded when their exact page or engine session ends.
