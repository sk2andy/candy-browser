# Candy Sync and smarter browsing

Move between Candy Browser on Android and your desktop browser without losing your place, while new
page tools and compatibility controls keep everyday browsing fluid.

![A synced desktop device selected as a Candy Browser profile](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/v0.33/docs/screenshots/candy-sync-device-profile.png)

## Your tabs, on every device

- Connect Candy Browser, Chromium, and Firefox through your self-hosted Candy Sync server.
- Open, navigate, reorder, pin, or close tabs from another device.
- Recover live changes automatically after reconnecting.

Learn more in [Cross-device sync](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.33/README.md#cross-device-sync).

## More capable browsing

- Temporarily allow third-party cookies for federated sign-in, or remember access for one site in its profile.
- Return to local history with Candy Recall and preview links from other apps before committing a tab.
- Translate pages, park configurable actions on either side of the address bar, and choose whether Candy animates at startup.

## Private by design

- Sync data is end-to-end encrypted before it leaves each device.
- Your self-hosted server stores ciphertext and cannot read tab titles or URLs.
- Private tabs remain outside persistent browser and synchronization state.

![Candy Sync setup with device identity and end-to-end encryption controls](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/v0.33/docs/screenshots/candy-sync-setup.png)

> Open **Settings → Synchronization** to connect your server and devices.
