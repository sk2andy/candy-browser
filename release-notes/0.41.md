# Video that stays in your hands

Candy Browser 0.41 introduces the optional Candy Player, connects live web playback to Android's
system controls, and adds more ways to shape the browser around your screen and habits.

## Watch videos with Candy Player

- Choose whether Candy Player appears from a button, works inline as well as fullscreen, takes over
  controls in supported website fullscreen, or starts when a supported video begins playing.
- Candy's inline controls stay with the original page video. A short tap hides or restores them,
  while fullscreen adds swipe-down dismissal, brightness control on the left, volume control on the
  right, responsive haptics, and the expressive wavy timeline.
- Enter Android picture-in-picture directly from a recognized inline video—fullscreen is no longer
  a required first step. Fullscreen and PiP returns restore the same page player and its position.
- The original Gecko session remains the only decoder and renderer; Candy does not open or download
  a second media stream.

> Candy Player currently targets visible top-frame HTML video in GeckoView. Cross-origin embeds,
> canvas-only players, DRM restrictions, and hostile site scripts remain unsupported or best effort.

Learn more in [Picture-in-picture and Candy Player](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41/docs/browsing/picture-in-picture.md#supported-paths).

## Keep playback connected to Android

- Eligible live Gecko playback can continue after Home or screen lock through a media-playback
  foreground service.
- Android's media notification and system surfaces control the exact playing session with Play,
  Pause, Stop, and Seek when the page reports a finite duration.
- Private tabs, locked profiles, closed pages, and stale navigations cannot publish metadata or keep
  control of the media session.

> Background playback keeps an existing Gecko session alive. It does not restore playback after the
> app process or that browser session is gone, and it stores no durable media URL or artwork.

Learn more in [Android system controls and background playback](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41/docs/browsing/picture-in-picture.md#android-system-controls-and-background-playback).

## Make the address bar yours

- Select the classic address bar or a new segmented style, then choose Theme, Dimmed, Graphite,
  Black, or a validated custom color. Candy preserves readable contrast across clear, frosted, and
  AMOLED surfaces.
- Configure address-bar long press for Link Peek, copy, share, download, or foreground/background
  tabs—including private tabs. Moving a parked address pill now requires a short hold, with haptic
  and wobble feedback that keeps quick overview swipes available.
- Settings are grouped by task, and expanded menus use more expressive motion and Material controls.

Learn more in [Appearance choices](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41/docs/browsing/appearance-and-settings.md#choices) and [address flow](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41/docs/browsing/address-actions-and-ui.md#address-flow).

## Use more room without losing context

- On windows at least 600 dp wide, the unfocused address field becomes a scrollable tab strip.
  Tabs show favicons when Candy can safely obtain them and fall back to title initials; pinned tabs
  cannot be closed from the strip.
- Tab previews are sharper on wide displays, while page gestures remain owned by web content.
- Favorites now support nested folders, moves and reordering. Folder tiles can use an emoji, an
  imported image, a favicon preview, or an automatic mosaic of their contents.

Learn more in [wide address-bar tabs](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41/docs/tabs-and-profiles/README.md#topic-lookup) and [favorite management](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41/docs/browsing/favorites.md#behavior-and-ownership).

## Strengthen privacy boundaries

- GeckoView can use strict encrypted DNS from a supported provider or a validated custom HTTPS
  endpoint. The setting remains visibly unavailable in the System WebView build, whose public API
  cannot provide the same contract.
- Toppings may declare top-frame, same-origin-frame, or all-matching-frame access. Your saved
  allowance can narrow that declaration, existing installs remain top-frame-only during migration,
  and Toppings never run in private tabs.
- Android shows one privacy-safe ongoing notification while private tabs remain in memory. Tapping
  it closes every private tab without opening Candy, and no private title or URL enters the
  notification.
- The bundled uBlock Origin has been updated to 1.75.0.

Learn more in [encrypted DNS](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41/docs/browsing/platform-engines.md#encrypted-dns), [Topping frame scopes](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41/docs/browsing/userscripts.md#supported-contract), and [private-tab notifications](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41/docs/tabs-and-profiles/previews-snooze-and-isolation.md#private-tab-notification).

## Smoother handoffs and recovery

- Web fullscreen now hides Candy chrome, restores page insets on exit, and keeps unrelated playback
  intact.
- Gecko pages stay visible while Android's Sharesheet is open, and failed pages can retry after the
  network returns.
- Candy download progress and completion notifications are restored. System WebView also captures
  user-opened, same-origin generated `blob:` popups before their temporary page closes.
- Gecko file uploads stage readable document-provider content safely for the active page.

Learn more in [runtime and navigation](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41/docs/browsing/runtime-and-navigation.md#web-media-fullscreen-and-picture-in-picture) and [download behavior](https://github.com/sk2andy/candy-browser/blob/v0.41/docs/browsing/downloads.md#behavior).

Private tabs remain memory-only. Candy Player state and Android media ownership are also memory-only
and are discarded when their exact page or engine session ends.
