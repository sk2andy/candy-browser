EasyList content-filter attribution
===================================

This application contains compiled copies of host-anchored network rules from
EasyList and EasyPrivacy and general element-hiding rules from the EasyList Cookie List.

Source: https://github.com/easylist/easylist
Authors: The EasyList authors (https://easylist.to/)
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported or later
License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/legalcode

The generic Adblock Plus element-hiding rules are modified by compiling them
into CSS. Host-anchored network rules are modified by compiling them into a sorted
plain-host list. The generated files are produced by scripts/update_cookie_banner_css.sh
and scripts/update_content_filter_hosts.sh.
