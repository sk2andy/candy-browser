EasyList content-filter attribution
===================================

This application contains compiled copies of host-anchored network rules and domain-specific
cosmetic rules from EasyList and EasyPrivacy, plus general element-hiding rules from the EasyList
Cookie List.

Source: https://github.com/easylist/easylist
Authors: The EasyList authors (https://easylist.to/)
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported or later
License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/legalcode

Generic cookie-banner element-hiding rules are modified by compiling them into CSS.
Domain-specific standard cosmetic rules, exclusions, and exceptions are compiled into Candy's
indexed cosmetic asset; unsupported operators are omitted. Host-anchored network rules are
modified into a sorted plain-host list. Generated files are produced by
scripts/update_cookie_banner_css.sh, scripts/update_content_filter_hosts.sh, and
scripts/update_easylist_cosmetic.sh.
