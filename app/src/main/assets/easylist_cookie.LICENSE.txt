EasyList Cookie List attribution
================================

This application contains a compiled copy of the general element-hiding rules
from the EasyList Cookie List.

Source: https://github.com/easylist/easylist
Authors: The EasyList authors (https://easylist.to/)
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported or later
License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/legalcode

The generic Adblock Plus element-hiding rules are modified by compiling them
into CSS. The generated CSS is produced by scripts/update_cookie_banner_css.sh.
