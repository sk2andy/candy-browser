## Required responsible use

Use of Pocket TTS must comply with applicable laws and the conditions shown on
the upstream model page. In particular, do not clone or impersonate a voice
without explicit, lawful consent. Do not use generated speech for fraud,
deception, harassment, privacy invasion, misinformation, or other harmful or
unauthorized activity.

Voice recordings have their own copyright, privacy, publicity, and consent
requirements. Release packs may include the official Pocket TTS default voices
under CC BY 4.0 with the attribution documented in
[`VOICE_ATTRIBUTION.md`](VOICE_ATTRIBUTION.md). For any additional recording,
only import audio that you created yourself or are authorized to use.

Changes made for the Android packs: each upstream recording is converted to
24 kHz mono 16-bit PCM WAV and renamed to its stable Pocket TTS voice ID. No
voice is synthesized, edited to impersonate another speaker, or mixed with a
user recording during packaging.

The names above are Pocket TTS catalog identifiers. Their inclusion does not
imply endorsement by Kyutai or by any recorded speaker. Other rights, including
privacy, publicity, and moral rights, may still apply. Use the voices lawfully
and do not present generated speech as an authentic recording of a real person.

Candy downloads the unmodified FP32 language packs from version 0.5.4 of
https://github.com/The-unknown-Shadowman/PocketTTS-Android-Engine
Archive and per-file SHA-256 checksums are pinned in PocketSpeechCatalog.
The model files and reference recordings are not bundled into the Candy APK.
